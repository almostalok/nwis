Well ID: OIL-SYN-007
Well Name: NWIS Precedent Well 07 (Barail Stuck Pipe Precedent)
Field: NWIS-DEMO-FIELD
Report Date: 06-OCT-2023
Rig: SYN-RIG-02
Current Depth: 3180 m MD / 3160 m TVD

INCIDENT SUMMARY:
At approximately 3180 m MD in the Barail Sandstone formation, an erratic 
increase in torque was observed, rising rapidly from 10.8 kN.m to 32.0 kN.m. 
Simultaneously, ROP decayed from 15.2 m/h to 0.4 m/h. The drill string subsequently 
became stuck off-bottom while attempting to make a connection.

GEOLOGICAL CORRELATION NOTE:
This interval strongly correlates with the carbonaceous shale boundary zone 
encountered in nearby well OIL-SYN-003 at 3210 m MD (distance approx. 0.45 km). 
Both events exhibit identical torque buildup precursors prior to pipe freeze.

MITIGATION & OUTCOME:
Circulation maintained at 1950 lpm. Spotted 10 m3 lubricating hydrocarbon pill. 
Applied repeated jarring with 40-tonne impacts for 36 hours. Drill string freed 
successfully without sidetrack requirement. Conditioned mud with 7% glycol shale 
inhibitor prior to resuming drilling.
