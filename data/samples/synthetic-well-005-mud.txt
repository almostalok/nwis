- Standpipe pressure dropped from 165 bar to 135 bar.

FLUID PROPERTIES AT TIME OF LOSS:
- Fluid Type: Dispersed Bentonite Lignite System

- Funnel Viscosity: 48 sec/qt
- Gel Strengths (10s/10m): 8/18 lb/100ft2
- API Filtrate: 6.5 ml/30min

TREATMENT & MITIGATION:
1. Driller halted rotation, picked off bottom to 2100 m casing shoe, stopped pumps.
2. Formulated 18 m3 LCM bridging blend:
   - 25 ppb fine & medium Calcium Carbonate
   - 15 ppb Nut Plug (walnut shell coarse)
   - 5 ppb synthetic cellulose fibers
3. Pumped LCM pill in two 9 m3 stages; allowed 4 hours static soak.
4. Slowly resumed circulation at 600 lpm, building up to 1800 lpm with 100% returns restored.
5. Adjusted continuous mud density to 1.12 sg to reduce hydrostatic overbalance.
